title: "From Linux Mint To Omarchy: Which One Was Better For Me?"

I tried multiple Linux distros. I started with Linux Mint and ended up with Omarchy but let's compare them.

## Ubuntu (In a VM with 2 GB RAM)

This was the first Linux distro I tried.

Not bad, huh, but I deleted it as soon as my laptop's SSD was full. I deleted it.

I didn't have a MacBook back then and I tried it on an ASUS VivoBook Flip (TP412UA).

that laptop had 4 GB of RAM and 128 SATA SSD.

## Linux Mint (good, but for beginners)

I started with Mint as a Windows escaper (I still have and need Windows though). Yeah, an operating system that any beginners would use. (I tried it on an old laptop (an HP ProBook 4330s))
It was good until I tried to use Hyprland. You know, Hyprland is for rolling release distros and trying it on a pointed-release one is a mess because it depends on latest technologies. So I switched to Fedora.

## Fedora

This one was the second distro I tried on a real computer. It was good. I installed ML4W dotfiles on it (I was a beginner back then). I had the mostly latest packages but I got tired of it very soon. I started using the KDE spin and then switched to Workstation but then, I switched to Arch (btw).

## Arch Linux (btw)

Arch Linux was the Linux I always wanted. Latest packages, Hyprland, end-4 dotfiles, everything but there was one thing: Updates that break the system. But I used it anyway and it made a real Linux enthusiast. Then I tried Manjaro.

## Manjaro

Manjaro was, eh..., not bad but it had one thing that made me happy: GUI app store.

Well, most people hate Pamac because it hides so many things from us. But I loved it anyway.

Then I switched to CachyOS.

## CachyOS

The thing that made CachyOS special was the performance... on an old laptop. but I got tired of it and switched back to Arch (btw).

## Ubuntu (again)

Ubuntu was good but I kept it for less than 4 hours and installed an operating system that made me impressed.

## NixOS

The good thing that impressed me about NixOS was the purely functional package manager called Nix. It was SOOOOO good and it really redefined the future of Linux. But I got back to Arch.

## Omarchy

Omarchy is a beautiful, opinionated Linux by DHH, the creator of Ruby on Rails.

It's so good. I used the manual installation and it was... good until it asked me about Limine. I bypassed it somehow by editing the installer (it was written in shell script, and I used GRUB instead).

The bad thing about it is that it's bloated. It reminds me of Windows 11. It has Basecamp, HEY, 1Password, Spotify, Discord (well, some actually use Discord, so that's not the problem for many users), ChatGPT and even Zoom. That's bad. But it's DHH's choice and we can't change it because it's an _omakase_ setup.

## Conclusion

I am now stuck between Omarchy, Arch and NixOS and I don't try anything else. But let me know what distro do you recommend to me.
